Pétale

A modular lamp in laser-cut acrylic: five translucent petals to reposition at will, switching from pendant to wall lamp and from direct to indirect light. Led from idea to customer delivery.

Context

A course project run as a team of four, from start to finish like an entrepreneurial adventure: find the idea, design it, prototype it, fund it, produce it and deliver it. Imposed constraint: the Quickstarter framework — max 6-month development, max 30-day campaign, main reward under €50, campaign video shot in one day, no paid advertising, no stretch goals, and the word "Quickstarter" in the title.

Approach

The product: 5 translucent orange acrylic petals (matte finish) mounted on a transparent frame. Deployed, the lamp measures 17 × 15 × 15 cm. Rearrange the petals by hand to turn it into a pendant, wall, or desk lamp, in direct or indirect mode.

Prototyping side: we first designed the petal and the clip-on socket mechanism, then machined the first parts. Laser cutting let us iterate quickly and precisely until the final design.

Assembly trick: the socket and bulb are deliberately excluded — buying them locally is cheaper and leaves the colour, cable length, bulb shape and colour temperature up to the buyer.

Results

  • 506 € pledged against a 150 € target, i.e. 337%.
  • 16 backers in 20 days (26 March – 15 April 2026).
  • Rewards: 1 Pétale at 19 €, 2 at 35 €, 3 at 50 €; worldwide shipping planned for December 2026.
  • All parts were prototyped and tested in-house before shipping, with particular attention to acrylic thickness variations.
