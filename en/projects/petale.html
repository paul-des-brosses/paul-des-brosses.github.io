Pétale

A modular lamp in laser-cut acrylic: five translucent petals you can reposition at will, to switch from pendant to wall lamp and from direct to indirect light. Taken from idea to customer delivery.

Context

A course project run in a team of four, from start to finish like an entrepreneurial venture: find the idea, design it, prototype it, fund it, produce it and deliver it.

An imposed constraint, the Quickstarter framework (created by Oscar Lhermitte): development in 6 months maximum, a campaign of 30 days maximum, main reward under $50, a campaign video shot in a single day, no paid advertising, no stretch goals, and the word “Quickstarter” in the title. Constraints that force constant prioritisation.

Approach

The product: 5 translucent orange acrylic petals (frosted finish) mounted on a transparent structure. When deployed, the lamp measures 17 × 15 × 15 cm. You rearrange the petals by hand to turn it into a pendant, a wall or desk lamp, direct or indirect lighting, upright, lying down, or even upside down.

On the prototyping side: we first designed the petal and the clip-on socket mechanism, then machined the first parts. Laser cutting let us iterate quickly and precisely up to the final design.

Assembly tip: the socket and bulb are deliberately not included. Buying them locally costs less and leaves you free to choose the colour, cable length, shape and colour temperature of the bulb.

Result

  • €506 raised against a €150 goal, i.e. 337%.
  • 16 backers in 20 days (26 March – 15 April 2026).
  • Rewards: 1 Pétale for €19, 2 Pétales for €35, 3 Pétales for €50; worldwide delivery planned for December 2026.
  • Every part was prototyped and tested in-house before shipping, with particular attention to variations in acrylic thickness.
